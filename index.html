How to lay out your cards
  • Stack the cards so each one covers the card above it, leaving only its name bar showing.
  • Up to about 10 cards per photo. Each name that shows counts as one copy.
  • The bottom card showing in full is fine.
  • Lay cards side by side in rows, for example 3 × 3, with the cards upright and lined up.
  • Keep small, even gaps between cards. Every card's name bar must be visible.
  • Each card counts once, so duplicates can sit in the grid too.
  • Portrait or landscape both work.
  • Hold the phone about 20–25 cm away. Much closer and the iPhone switches to its macro lens, which can blur the edges. To fill the picture with the cards, use the camera's 2× button instead of moving closer.
  • Flash off, and avoid glare on sleeves. Less table around the cards makes scans quicker.

Settings

Claude for deck building

With a Claude API key, decks are built by Claude, which reads every card's text and plans around your cards. Each deck costs a few cents on your Anthropic account. Without a key, the built-in builder is used.

Get a key at platform.claude.com → Settings → API keys. It is saved only on this phone and sent only to Anthropic.

Card list

Adds names from new sets using Scryfall, so scans recognize them. Needs internet.

Prices

Euro prices from Cardmarket, via Scryfall. They change once a day and are refreshed when you open your collection online; the last prices are kept for offline use. A copy with a recorded set gets that printing's price, otherwise the card's usual printing.

Your data

Your collections and decks are stored only on this phone. Make a backup now and then: copy it and keep it in a note or email it to yourself. To restore, paste it into "Paste a card list" on the Scan tab; each collection is recreated by name.
