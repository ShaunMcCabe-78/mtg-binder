Add cards from photos

Stack your cards so only the names show, then take a photo. The names are read and checked against every Magic card on your phone, and you confirm the list before anything is added.

Loading the card list…

The app finds which way the names run by itself. If it gets it wrong, tap a photo to turn it until the names read left to right.

Reading your cards…

Check what was found

Tap a name to correct it. Cards marked check were a close match, and unknown isn't a card name yet.

Text that didn't match a card (0)

Usually stray text from the artwork. If one of these is a card, tap the right name to add it.

How to lay out your cards
  • Stack the cards so each one covers the card above it, leaving only its name bar showing.
  • Up to about 10 cards per photo. Each name that shows counts as one copy.
  • Portrait or landscape both work.
  • Hold the phone about 20–25 cm away at 1×. Much closer and the iPhone switches to its macro lens, which can blur the edges. Flash off, so sleeves don't glare.

Claude for deck building

With a Claude API key, decks are built by Claude, which reads every card's text and plans around your cards. Each deck costs a few cents on your Anthropic account. Without a key, the built-in builder is used.

Get a key at console.anthropic.com → API Keys. It is saved only on this phone and sent only to Anthropic.

Card list

Adds names from new sets using Scryfall, so scans recognize them. Needs internet.

Your data

Your collection and decks are stored only on this phone. Make a backup now and then: copy the list and keep it in a note or email it to yourself. To restore, paste it into "Paste a card list" on the Scan tab.
